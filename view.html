Lexicon Earth / Viewer

Open the atlas and site pages from this folder. One place to jump into the live globe or the publisher pages.

For the full atlas (ES modules, GeoJSON, service worker), serve the folder locally, then open this page or index.html:

cd "Lexicon Earth" && python3 -m http.server 8080
then visit http://localhost:8080/view.html
